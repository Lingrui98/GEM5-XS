## Interpretation

F1 is wired into the controller: the CSV contains non-zero `new_utility_*`
columns derived from phase deltas, and the decision predicate sees the F1
values. The controller correctly holds because MBTB miss-rate pressure is
higher than TAGE mispredict-rate pressure for almost the entire smoke.

This is not the v3 "TAGE higher but below 0.10" branch, so the 0.05 threshold
diagnostic is not appropriate. The result is the v3 hard STOP case:

> If Phase B-rev Step 2 shows TAGE mispredict rate LOWER than MBTB miss rate on
> gcc_pp_O2, STOP and escalate, likely a stat aggregation bug.

Likely explanation to investigate before any Phase C run:

- MBTB `predMiss` counts a broader BTB lookup/miss surface than the TAGE
  conditional-update denominator, so the two rates are not directly comparable
  as a capacity-pressure utility.
- If F1 is retained, MBTB utility may need a denominator aligned to conditional
  branches that can actually benefit from TAGE capacity, or the design should
  move to F2 ATD marginal MPKI.
